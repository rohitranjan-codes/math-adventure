Why November

The monsoon is gone, the crowds haven't arrived.

South India dries out and cools down in November. Here's how each stop on the map feels — and the one region to leave for another trip.

Getting there

Frankfurt or Zurich to Bengaluru

Lufthansa flies nonstop from Frankfurt. From Zurich you connect once — via Frankfurt, Munich or a Gulf hub. Fly in together, or meet at Kempegowda International Airport.

🛬 Arriving at Bengaluru (BLR)

Immigration: e-Visa printout + return ticket SIM counters (Airtel / Jio) in arrivals ATMs & currency exchange before exit Taxi to city: 45–75 min · ₹1,000–1,500 (≈ €10–15) Vayu Vajra airport bus: ₹300

Most European flights land between 01:00 and 03:00. Book your first hotel from the night before so you can check in immediately, use the prepaid taxi counter or Uber pickup zone, and plan nothing before lunch on day one.
